\section{Notation summary}
For the reader's convenience, this appendix collects the principal notation used in the manuscript.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.70\textwidth}}
\caption{Main notation used in the fractional stochastic transportation model.}\\
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning\\
\midrule
\endfirsthead
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning\\
\midrule
\endhead
$I$ & Set of suppliers.\\
$J$ & Set of customers or demand zones.\\
$K$ & Set of transportation modes.\\
$T=\{1,\dots,N\}$ & Set of planning periods.\\
$\Omega$ & Set of demand scenarios.\\
$p_{\omega}$ & Probability of scenario $\omega$.\\
$S_{it}$ & Supply available at supplier $i$ in period $t$.\\
$d_{jt}^{\omega}$ & Demand of customer $j$ in period $t$ under scenario $\omega$.\\
$c_{ijkt}$ & Unit transportation cost on supplier--customer--mode arc $(i,j,k)$ in period $t$.\\
$e_{ijkt}$ & Unit emissions factor on arc $(i,j,k)$ in period $t$.\\
$Q_k$ & Capacity coefficient of transportation mode $k$.\\
$f_k$ & Fixed activation cost of mode $k$.\\
$h_j$ & Cost coefficient associated with the effective state at customer $j$.\\
$\pi_j$ & Unit shortage penalty for customer $j$.\\
$A$ & Initial allowance endowment in the carbon market.\\
$p^b$ & Carbon-allowance buying price.\\
$p^s$ & Carbon-allowance selling price.\\
$\rho_j$ & Dissipation or deterioration parameter in the fractional state equation.\\
$\gamma_j$ & Minimum required service fraction for customer $j$.\\
$\alpha$ & Fractional order in the Caputo derivative, with $0<\alpha<1$.\\
$\beta$ & Confidence level used in the CVaR risk measure.\\
$\lambda$ & Weight assigned to the CVaR term in the objective.\\
$y_k$ & Binary variable indicating whether mode $k$ is activated.\\
$x_{ijkt}^{\omega}$ & Shipment from supplier $i$ to customer $j$ by mode $k$ in period $t$ under scenario $\omega$.\\
$z_{jt}^{\omega}$ & Effective inventory--service state of customer $j$ in period $t$ under scenario $\omega$.\\
$u_{jt}^{\omega}$ & Shortage of customer $j$ in period $t$ under scenario $\omega$.\\
$b^{\omega}$ & Carbon allowances purchased under scenario $\omega$.\\
$s^{\omega}$ & Carbon allowances sold under scenario $\omega$.\\
$C^{\omega}$ & Total scenario-dependent operational cost.\\
$E^{\omega}$ & Total scenario-dependent emissions.\\
$\eta$ & Auxiliary CVaR threshold variable.\\
$\xi_{\omega}$ & Auxiliary excess variable for CVaR linearization.\\
$a_r$ & L1 discretization coefficient, $a_r=(r+1)^{1-\alpha}-r^{1-\alpha}$.\\
$\Delta t$ & Length of one planning interval.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The notation is deliberately kept compact so that the model can be adapted to richer network structures without changing its conceptual core.
